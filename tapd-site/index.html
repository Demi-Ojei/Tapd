Why boutique hotels switch

The card that stops working
the moment it meets your phone.

Your phone kills the key card

Put a hotel key card in the same pocket as your phone and the magnetic stripe wipes itself. Guests end up back at the front desk — on average more than once per stay. That's lost time for everyone.

No more 2am lockouts

A dead card at midnight means a panicked guest and a late-night phone call. With Tapd the key lives on their phone — the one thing they never leave in the room — and it stays active until check-out.

No plastic. Full stop.

Every digital key means one less plastic card thrown away. A 20-room hotel at normal occupancy goes through around 5,000 cards a year. Tapd brings that to zero.

Platform capabilities

Everything a hotel needs
already built in.

Apple Wallet

Room keys go straight into Apple Wallet. Guests tap their iPhone or Apple Watch at the door — no app to download, no login, nothing to figure out.

Google Wallet

Android guests get the exact same experience. Google Wallet, same tap, same speed. It doesn't matter which phone your guest carries — Tapd works with both.

Staff master keys

Give housekeeping, maintenance, and managers digital keys scoped only to the areas they need. Every use is logged, and you can revoke any key instantly.

Connects to your PMS

Tapd hooks into your existing booking system — Cloudbeds, Opera, Apaleo, and more. When a guest checks in, a key is issued automatically. When they check out, it's gone.

Full access log

Every time a door opens or a key is used, it's recorded with a timestamp and the device that used it. You always know who went where and when.

Kill a key instantly

Guest lost their phone? Early check-out? Security concern? Pull up the dashboard and revoke any key in one tap. It stops working immediately, everywhere.
